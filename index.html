How to Play

Objective

Destroy every brick on the screen without letting the ball fall past your paddle. Each level awards up to 3 stars based on how many lives you have left.

Controls

Touch / Mouse: Drag anywhere on the play area to move the paddle. Tap to launch the ball.

Keyboard: ← → or A / D to move. Space or Enter to launch / pause. Esc to pause.

Pause: Tap the ❚❚ button in the top-left during play.

Brick Types

Colored bricks — Normal, 1–4 hits. Pip count shows remaining HP.

Grey metal bricks — Tougher (double HP) and never drop power-ups.

Red explosive bricks — Explode on destruction, damaging nearby bricks.

Crown boss bricks — High HP, appear every 10th level. Focus fire!

Power-Ups

↔Wide — Bigger paddle for a while
●●●Multi — Splits into extra balls
⏱Slow — Slows the ball
⚡Laser — Paddle fires automatically
◎Sticky — Ball sticks to paddle
♥Life — Extra life

Tips

• Chain brick hits without touching the paddle to build a combo multiplier — more score per brick.

• The ball speeds up with each level, so aim for the top corners.

• Earn coins by clearing levels and finishing the daily challenge. Spend 50 coins to continue after a game over.
